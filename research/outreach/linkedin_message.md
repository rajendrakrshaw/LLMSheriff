# LinkedIn / email outreach template

**Send ~20 thoughtful messages. Quality > quantity. Target: 3–5 yeses.**

## Priority targets
1. PhD students (LLMs / agents / NLP / ML systems)
2. Research engineers
3. ML engineers
4. Professors (lower response rate — fewer messages)

## Message (copy and personalize first line)

> Hi [Name],
>
> I'm conducting a small research study on behavioral state inference for autonomous AI agents as part of the EPFL AI Summer Research Program.
>
> I'm looking for a few volunteers with AI/software experience to label 40 short execution traces. It takes around 20–30 minutes.
>
> The data is synthetic and anonymized. I'd really appreciate your help, and I'd be happy to share the paper after submission.
>
> Thank you!

## When someone says yes — send immediately
1. **Preferred:** link to the web quiz: `https://llmsheriff.rajendra.dev/study`
2. Backup: `PARTICIPANT_SHEET.md` + zip `LLMSheriff_annotator_packet.zip`
3. Deadline ask: e.g. “Could you finish by [date ~10–14 days]?”

## Do not send
- `private/gold_labels.json`
- Full paper (optional later)
- Export token / admin URLs
