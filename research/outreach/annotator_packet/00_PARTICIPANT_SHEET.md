# Participant information sheet

**Study:** Behavioral state labeling for autonomous AI agent traces  
**Programme:** EPFL Europe AI Summer Research (exploratory)  
**Time:** ~20–30 minutes  
**Traces:** 40 short synthetic execution logs (no personal data)

## What is LLMSheriff?

A research prototype that maps agent execution traces to coarse behavioral states
(e.g. Waiting, Stalled, Abandoned) using rules and an LLM, and treats agreement /
disagreement as a confidence / review signal.

## What you will do

1. Read a one-page labeling rubric.
2. For each of 40 short traces, choose **one** state:
   - Completed · Recovering · Waiting · Stalled · Abandoned
3. Optional: confidence (1–5) and a short note if unsure.

You will **not** see “gold” labels. There are no right answers you must match—we measure
whether people apply the vocabulary consistently.

## Helpful background

AI/ML, LLM apps, agents, or software engineering experience helps. You do **not** need
to be a published researcher.

## How data will be used

Labels are used only for this research paper (inter-rater agreement and discussion of
ambiguous cases). Synthetic traces contain no real user data. Results may appear in an
anonymized conference-style paper for the EPFL programme.
